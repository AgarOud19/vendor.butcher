Kashiwabara Wagyu, from the farm to our counter

In 2018 this shop became the first in Mongolia to handle Japanese A5 Wagyu. The beef comes from Kashiwabara Farm, and it arrives as it left — graded, traceable, and cut here rather than somewhere else.

A5 is the top of the Japanese grading scale. The marbling is the point: it melts into the meat as it cooks, which is why a small piece is enough and why you should not treat it like an ordinary steak.

  • Single-farm provenance Kashiwabara Farm, Japan
  • Graded A5 The top grade on the Japanese scale
  • Cut to order By the gram, in front of you
